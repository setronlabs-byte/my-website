Overview

IT Consultancy for businesses that need it to work

Technology decisions are expensive to reverse. We give you an independent, engineering-led view on architecture, tooling, and vendors, so you choose with clear information rather than a sales pitch.

Engagements range from a short review to an ongoing advisory arrangement, and every recommendation is delivered in writing.

What you can expect

  • A written assessment you can share with stakeholders
  • A prioritised roadmap with realistic effort and cost ranges
  • Vendor and tooling choices compared on your criteria
  • Reduced technical risk before major spending

What we deliver

Everything included in it consultancy

Engagements are scoped around the items below. We agree which apply to you in writing before work begins.

01

Technology audit

Review of your current systems, code, hosting, and processes, with findings ranked by impact.

02

Architecture review

Assessment of scalability, reliability, and maintainability of a planned or existing design.

03

Vendor & tool selection

Structured comparison of platforms and suppliers against your requirements and budget.

04

Digital roadmap

A phased plan connecting technology investment to business goals.

05

Build vs. buy analysis

Clear cost and risk comparison of custom development, off-the-shelf, and hybrid options.

06

Project oversight

Technical review of work delivered by other vendors or internal teams.

How we work

A clear process from first call to support

The same engineers who scope your project build and support it.

  1. Briefing

    We agree on the questions to answer, the decision deadline, and who needs to be involved.

  2. Investigation

    Interviews, system review, and research into your options.

  3. Analysis

    Findings tested against your goals, constraints, and budget.

  4. Recommendation

    A written report and walkthrough with clear, prioritised next steps.

  5. Follow-through

    Optional support while the recommendations are put into practice.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you recommend your own services?

Only when they are the best fit. Reviews are independent, and you are free to act on the recommendations with any provider.

How long does a review take?

A focused review usually takes one to three weeks depending on the size of the system.

What will I receive at the end?

A written report with findings, priorities, and recommendations, plus a walkthrough session with your team.

Is advisory available on an ongoing basis?

Yes. A monthly arrangement gives you a technical sounding board for decisions as they arise.

Ready to discuss your it consultancy project?

Tell us what you need. We will reply with questions and an outline of how we would approach it.
