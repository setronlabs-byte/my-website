Overview

Cybersecurity for businesses that need it to work

Security problems are cheaper to fix before an incident than after one. We assess your applications and infrastructure, explain the risks in plain language, and help you fix what matters most first.

Our reports prioritise by business impact, so your team knows what to fix this week and what can wait.

What you can expect

  • A prioritised list of weaknesses with clear remediation steps
  • Reduced exposure of customer and business data
  • Evidence and documentation to support customer and audit requests
  • A team that understands the risks in its own systems

What we deliver

Everything included in cybersecurity

Engagements are scoped around the items below. We agree which apply to you in writing before work begins.

01

Security assessments

Review of applications, servers, and configuration for common and critical weaknesses.

02

Vulnerability testing

Authorised testing of web applications and infrastructure, with reproducible findings.

03

Threat modelling

Structured analysis of how your system could be attacked and what to protect first.

04

Compliance support

Gap analysis and documentation toward standards such as ISO 27001 and data-protection requirements.

05

Secure development guidance

Code review practices, dependency checks, and secure design guidance for your developers.

06

Incident readiness

Response plans, access reviews, and backup checks so you can act quickly if something goes wrong.

How we work

A clear process from first call to support

The same engineers who scope your project build and support it.

  1. Scope & authorisation

    Agree what is in scope, testing rules, and timing in writing.

  2. Assessment

    Systematic review and testing of the agreed systems.

  3. Risk rating

    Findings rated by likelihood and business impact.

  4. Report & walkthrough

    Clear report with fixes, explained to technical and non-technical staff.

  5. Retest

    Verification that the fixes work.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will testing disrupt our systems?

We agree rules of engagement beforehand and test carefully, using staging environments where possible.

Do you fix the issues as well?

We can. The report is written so your own team can fix issues, or we can implement the fixes.

Can you help us prepare for a customer security questionnaire?

Yes. We review your current controls and help you document them accurately.

How often should we assess?

At least annually and after major changes. Many clients also test before each significant release.

Tell us what you need. We will reply with questions and an outline of how we would approach it.
