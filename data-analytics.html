Overview

Data Analytics for businesses that need it to work

Most businesses already have the data they need, scattered across spreadsheets, databases, and tools. We bring it together, clean it, and present it in dashboards your team will actually use.

We focus on a small set of metrics tied to real decisions rather than dashboards that nobody opens.

What you can expect

  • One trusted source for key business numbers
  • Reports that update automatically instead of by hand
  • Faster, better-informed weekly and monthly decisions
  • Staff who can explore the data without writing code

How we work

A clear process from first call to support

The same engineers who scope your project build and support it.

  1. Questions first

    Identify the decisions you need to make and the metrics behind them.

  2. Data audit

    Locate sources, assess quality, and agree on definitions.

  3. Pipeline & model

    Build the automated flow and structure the data for reporting.

  4. Dashboard build

    Design views with your team and test them on real data.

  5. Handover & refine

    Train users and adjust based on how the reports are used.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

We only have spreadsheets. Is that enough?

Yes. Spreadsheets are a common starting point, and we can automate their consolidation and move you to a more robust setup over time.

Which dashboard tool will you use?

We recommend a tool that fits your budget and existing software, such as Power BI or a custom web dashboard.

How do you keep the data accurate?

We agree metric definitions with you, validate outputs against known figures, and add automated checks.

Can our team maintain the reports afterward?

Yes. Handover includes documentation and training, and we are available for support if needed.
