What it is and why it matters

About the project

Energy bills tell you what you spent, but not why. Facility teams need to know which building is drifting upward, which equipment uses the most and when demand is costing the most.

GridSense answers those questions in plain language. It compares readings with each location’s normal pattern, highlights what looks unusual and shows what a different tariff or more solar could mean in rupees.

Key benefits

  • Cut avoidable cost by finding the buildings and equipment that use the most
  • Spot problems early when a reading breaks from normal behaviour
  • Reduce peak charges by seeing exactly when demand is highest
  • Plan with confidence using a 30-day consumption forecast
  • Report on sustainability with emissions and renewable share
