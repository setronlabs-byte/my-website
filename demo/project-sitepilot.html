What it is and why it matters

About the project

Construction projects succeed or slip on coordination: who is on site, what material has arrived, which task is behind and whether spending still matches the plan.

SitePilot puts those answers in one place. Open any project to see its details, progress through each stage, and how the budget is split across categories, then check tasks, crews and materials without calling the site.

Key benefits

  • Stay on schedule with a stage-wise timeline from foundation to finishing
  • Control cost with the budget shown by category
  • Spot delays early on the task board
  • Avoid shortages with low-stock warnings on key materials
  • Know what is happening through daily site updates
Features

Everything inside SitePilot

Each module is a page in the demo. Open the live version to click through them.

Dashboard

Active projects, key numbers and items that need attention today.

Projects

Every project with its progress, budget, information and timeline.

Tasks

A board that shows work as Not Started, In Progress, Delayed or Completed.

Workers

Your crew with roles and site assignments.

Materials

Cement, steel, sand, bricks and more, with usage and low-stock alerts.

Equipment

Cranes, excavators, mixers and more with status (in use, idle or in maintenance) and working hours.

Expenses

Spending recorded against each project and rolled into its budget.

Site Updates

Daily field updates so the office always sees the real status.

Reports

Progress, cost and resource summaries to share with stakeholders.
