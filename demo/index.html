    Good to know before you come

    Do I need an appointment?

    Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free, but booking guarantees your time with your chosen stylist. Weekends fill up first.

    Can I change or cancel a booking?

    Yes. Changes and cancellations are free up to four hours before your slot. After that, we may charge 25% of the service total.

    I have sensitive skin or allergies. Can you help?

    Tell us in the notes when you book. We offer a free patch test for colour and skin treatments, and we can switch to fragrance-free products.

    Can we book together as a couple or family?

    Yes. Book one visit and mention the extra guests in the notes, or make separate bookings with the same time so we seat you side by side.

    How do memberships work?

    You pick a plan, we bill you each month or each year, and your included services appear when you book. Unused services do not roll over beyond one month.
