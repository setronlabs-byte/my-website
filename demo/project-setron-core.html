Overview

What it is and why it matters

About the project

Most growing businesses run on a patchwork of spreadsheets, messages and separate apps. Numbers disagree, stock surprises arrive late and nobody has the full picture.

Setron Core brings everything together. An order updates the customer record, adjusts stock and shows up in your reports, so the team works from one set of facts instead of chasing updates.

Key benefits

  • Know today’s position at a glance, from sales to open tasks
  • Never run out unexpectedly with clear stock levels and low-stock warnings
  • Understand your customers through order history in one place
  • Control spending by seeing expenses by category
  • Keep the team aligned with shared tasks and owners
